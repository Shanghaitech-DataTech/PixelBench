Abstract摘要

Pixel art is widely used in games and digital content creation, requiring readable forms, coherent spatial relationships, and harmonious colors under limited resolution and palette constraints. Language models show growing promise in visual creation, yet their pixel art creation capabilities lack systematic evaluation. We introduce PixelBench, formalizing text-to-image generation and instruction-guided editing for pixel art and providing a benchmark for these tasks. We further present a creation environment in which language model agents invoke drawing tools through code to generate and edit pixel art. We assess the quality of generated artwork through human evaluation and employ a multimodal large language model as a judge, validating the agreement between its automated assessments and human preferences. We compare language models and configurations across diverse subjects and canvas sizes. In our experiments, GPT-6 Astra achieves both the highest average judge score and the best average human rank, highlighting the potential of general-purpose language models for pixel art creation. We release the tasks, all artworks, the human rankings, and the judge protocol.

像素画广泛用于游戏和数字内容创作，其制作要求在有限的分辨率和调色板约束下组织清晰的形态、连贯的空间关系与协调的色彩。语言模型在视觉创作中展现出新的潜力，但其像素画创作能力仍缺乏系统评估。我们提出 PixelBench，规范化像素画的文本生成与指令编辑两类任务，并为其构建评测基准。我们进一步提供一个创作环境，语言模型 Agent 在其中通过代码调用绘图工具来生成和编辑像素画。我们通过人类评价衡量生成作品的质量，并采用多模态大语言模型作为评审，验证其自动评价与人类偏好的一致性。我们在多种题材与画幅上比较了不同语言模型及其配置，实验中 GPT-6 Astra 同时获得最高的平均 Judge 分数和最好的人类平均名次，显示出通用语言模型用于像素画创作的潜力。我们公开全部题目、所有作品、人类排名与评审协议。

1Task suite任务套件

100 tasks, each written and reviewed by people. The 75 text-to-image tasks span seven subjects on canvases from 16×16 to 256×256; the 25 editing tasks start from an existing artwork: 13 instruction edits, 10 animations and 2 multiview turnarounds.

100 道题目，均由人撰写和审核。75 道文生图题目覆盖七类题材，画幅从 16×16 到 256×256；25 道编辑题目从一幅已有作品出发，包括 13 道指令编辑、10 道动画和 2 道多视角。

2Creation environment创作环境

Every model receives the same prompt, tools and budget. It writes a Python program against a pixel-art drawing API, runs it in a sandbox, looks at the result and revises, within 25 source writes for generation and 15 for editing. The prompt carries no pixel-art technique guidance and no exemplar artwork.

所有模型使用相同的提示词、工具与预算：模型针对像素画绘图 API 编写 Python 程序，在沙箱中运行，查看结果后再修改；生成题最多写入源码 25 次，编辑题 15 次。提示词中不含像素画技法指导，也不含示例作品。

3Evaluation protocol评价协议

Ten raters with professional art backgrounds ranked all 1,200 artworks of the twelve main configurations. A calibrated multimodal judge, GPT-6 Astra at reasoning effort xhigh, scores every artwork from 0 to 100; under leave-one-out, its agreement with the raters' consensus falls within the range of the ten raters.

10 位具有专业美术背景的人类评分者对 12 个主配置的全部 1,200 件作品逐题排序。经过校准的多模态评审 GPT-6 Astra（思考强度 xhigh）为每件作品打 0–100 分；在留一法比较下，它与评分者共识的一致性落在十位评分者的范围之内。

    Protocol评测协议 — how these numbers were produced这些数字如何得到

    The judge评审模型

    GPT-6 Astra at reasoning effort xhigh scores each artwork independently. A call carries the task statement, the canvas and color requirement, a same-task reference image and one anonymized artwork (an editing call also shows the original), and returns one integer from 0 to 100.

    GPT-6 Astra（思考强度 xhigh）独立地为每件作品打分。每次调用只包含题目陈述、画幅与颜色要求、一张同题参考图和一件匿名作品（编辑题另附原图），输出一个 0–100 的整数。

    The artwork gets a transparent margin of one sixteenth on its left and top and is magnified with nearest-neighbor sampling to 512 pixels on the longer side. Multi-frame results are laid out in order as one image, and material tiles are repeated 2×2.

    作品先在左侧和上方加上画幅 1/16 的透明边距，再以最近邻采样放大到长边 512 像素。多帧结果按顺序拼成一张图，材质贴图以 2×2 平铺展示。

    Human ranking人类排序

    Ten raters with professional art backgrounds, mainly in art and game design, ranked the twelve artworks of each task from best to worst on visual quality and instruction following. Artworks carried random labels and appeared in a random order for each rater, and animations played in a loop.

    10 位具有专业美术背景（主要来自美术与游戏设计方向）的人类评分者，按视觉质量和指令遵循程度对每道题的 12 件作品从好到差排序。作品以随机标签匿名，每位评分者看到的初始顺序随机，动画循环播放。

    Eight raters also saw a reference for each task. The leaderboard of the two who did not correlates with that of the other eight at Spearman ρ = .

    其中 8 位评分者同时看到每题的参考图。另外 2 位未看参考图的评分者给出的榜单，与其余 8 位的榜单 Spearman 相关系数为 ρ = 。

    Creation environment创作环境

    The model delivers the artwork as a Python program. Every run starts from the task input in a fresh sandboxed process without network access, and view(canvas) returns the magnified canvas to the model as an image.

    模型以 Python 程序交付作品。每次运行都在新的沙箱进程中从题目输入开始，无网络访问；view(canvas) 把放大后的画布以图像形式返回给模型。

    The drawing API offers layers, pixels, lines, shapes, SVG paths, flood fill, mirroring, selections that flip, resize or rotate, dithering and text, all without anti-aliasing. The budget counts source writes: 25 for generation and 15 for editing.

    绘图 API 提供图层、像素、直线、几何形状、SVG 路径、填充、镜像、可翻转缩放旋转的选区、抖动和文字，均不做抗锯齿。预算按源码写入次数计：生成题 25 次，编辑题 15 次。
