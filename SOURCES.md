# Sources of the editing inputs

Each editing task starts from existing pixel art; three of the inputs were drawn by the authors. The table
cites the work each task uses, as the task cards of the paper's appendix do. Each task takes at most three
frames of a single work. The inputs and the frames derived from them appear here as citations: they document
what each task asks for and what the submissions were asked to change, and they are reproduced for no other
purpose. Third-party works remain under their original terms; the license column gives what the source
states (an empty cell means the source states none). If you hold rights in one of these works and would prefer
it not to appear, please contact us: we will replace the affected task with a similar new task and re-evaluate
it.
